Overview

Sound built
for the big screen

A Version of Me is a short film produced by Retroframe, for which I handled the complete sound post production pipeline from scratch — from raw location sound all the way through to the final cinema-ready mix and master.

The project required managing every layer of the audio: dialogue clarity, immersive sound design, Foley performance, original score composition, and a final mix balanced for theatrical playback. The score was written to serve the film's emotional arc, with themes crafted to support the narrative at each turning point.

This project is currently in final production stages. A draft cut of the sound post is complete and under review.

What I Did

04 Disciplines
🎙
Dialogue Editing
Cleaned and edited all production dialogue for clarity and consistency. Handled noise reduction, breath control, and sync corrections to ensure intelligible, broadcast-ready speech.
🔊
SFX Design & Foley
Designed the sound effects layer and recorded original Foley to replace and enhance on-set sound. Every footstep, texture, and ambient detail was crafted to serve the visual rhythm of the film.
🎼
Film Score Composition
Composed and produced the original film score. Developed thematic motifs aligned with the characters and emotional beats, using cinematic orchestration and electronic texture.
🎚
Final Mix & Master
Delivered the final mix balancing dialogue, Foley, SFX, ambience, and music across all stems. Mastered for cinema output with attention to loudness, dynamics, and theatrical playback standards.
