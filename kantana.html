Overview

Post Sound
Editorial

Kantana Group is one of Thailand's leading entertainment and media conglomerates, home to full-scale production facilities spanning film, television, and post production. The Original Sound Post department handles all audio post work for Kantana's in-house productions — from broadcast drama series and reality television to documentary features.

As an Editor Intern within Original Sound Post, I worked across the full editorial pipeline under the supervision of professional mixers, preparing and delivering audio for broadcast. Each project demanded precision, speed, and a deep understanding of how sound interacts with picture at every stage of post production.

Working alongside senior engineers and fellow editors, I contributed to productions spanning nationally broadcast drama series, reality television, and documentary content — building a foundation in professional editorial workflow that connects directly to final mix delivery.
