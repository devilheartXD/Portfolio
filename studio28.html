Studio 28 is a professional recording studio specialising in music recording, mixing, and mastering. As an intern Assistant Engineer, I work directly alongside senior engineers on live recording sessions — from intimate solo recordings to full-scale orchestral productions.

My responsibilities span two key areas: session support for Chief Recording Engineer Ong, where I handle microphone placement for orchestras, grand piano, and ensemble recording; and project editing support for Henry Watkin, a professional Music Mixing & Mastering Engineer, where I assist in preparing sessions ahead of the final mix.

This internship has given me hands-on exposure to professional-grade signal chains, room acoustics for orchestral recording, and the meticulous preparation required for a world-class mix session.
